# ChurnAutopsy Growth Agent

Private, single-owner B2B SaaS lead research workspace for ChurnAutopsy. Phase 1 finds US software companies with a target of 2–50 employees, explains fit signals, and supports human save/reject decisions. Demo data is fictional and clearly labeled. Campaigns and Inbox describe future capabilities; sending is disabled. The existing ChurnAutopsy production project is out of scope.

The primary task is reviewing a compact company list and understanding why each company may fit. Dark theme, responsive dashboard, accessible controls, and honest missing-data states are required. Avoid invented performance claims, fake activity charts, or presenting uncertain employee buckets as exact counts.
