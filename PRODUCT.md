# ChurnAutopsy Growth Agent

Private, single-owner B2B SaaS growth workspace for ChurnAutopsy, extending company discovery and evidence-based qualification into bounded automation. The implemented workspace includes Overview, Companies, Qualified Leads, Contacts, Campaigns, Email Sequences, Inbox, Opportunities, Automation Settings, and Usage and Costs, alongside existing workspace Settings. Company save/reject decisions remain separate from qualification and outreach eligibility. The existing ChurnAutopsy production application, database, deployment, and credentials must never be accessed or modified.

The workflow covers scheduled discovery, public-website evidence, decision-maker lookup, email verification, consent assessment, bounded sequences, Gmail reply polling, unsubscribe suppression, and opportunity tracking. A persistent worker can continue independently of the owner's computer when deployed with dedicated Supabase storage and private hosting. Queues, credit reservations, outbox state, approvals, and append-only audit history support low-volume, single-owner operation. Emergency pause stops new jobs and dispatch; ambiguous deliveries require reconciliation and are never automatically resent.

Dry-run is the default: fictional, clearly labeled records and simulated messages use providers that make no external requests, even when credentials exist. Demo storage is in memory and resets on API restart; demo journeys use manual job ticks. Persistent background processing requires live Supabase storage, which can still run the isolated dry-run workflow.

Paid Hunter calls require explicit cost approval, a server gate, and nonzero budgets. Real sending is off by default and requires an approved campaign policy, separate server and owner-interface gates, configured Gmail authorization, a verified mailbox/unsubscribe test, and eligible contacts. Test messages also require explicit authorization. Eligibility requires current, documented express consent specifically for ChurnAutopsy audit outreach and known supported jurisdiction; a saved company, public address, fit score, or verified inbox never establishes consent. Each contact receives at most one lifetime sequence with two follow-ups; replies and opt-outs stop further outreach. Optional model assistance is local-only and off by default; it cannot override evidence rules or establish consent.

These capabilities are implemented, not a claim of live activation. External integrations are contract-tested with mocks. The owner still supplies the additive migration, private hosting and monitoring, sender details, policy review and valid consent evidence, approved budgets, Google OAuth authorization, public HTTPS unsubscribe routing, controlled test recipient, and explicit live activation. Mailbox polling is not instantaneous; in-flight sends cannot be recalled. See [the activation guide](docs/activation-guide.md) for configuration, operating limits, and remaining external checks.

The primary task is understanding the next operational action and its evidence, permissions, costs, or blockers while retaining deliberate company review. Preserve the established dark theme, responsive dashboard, accessible controls, and honest missing-data states. Avoid invented performance claims, fake activity charts, or presenting uncertain employee buckets as exact counts.
